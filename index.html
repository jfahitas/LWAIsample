Your call, before any analysis

What the numbers couldn’t see

Specialized team: where the time actually goes
Billed workload, 42%Drills, certification, standby

This team is one of the few groups trained to restore critical systems during a major outage. Most of its time goes to staying ready, work that never appears in a billing cycle and so reads as idle. The contingency hasn’t been activated in four years.

And it isn’t that simple

No one has ever tested whether six people are actually needed. The team lead strongly opposes cuts and has both real context and a personal stake. The AI’s numbers aren’t wrong. They’re incomplete.
